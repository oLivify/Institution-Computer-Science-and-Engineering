About the Institution

Our Mission & Vision

Baron-Bryan Institution of Computer Science and Engineering is dedicated to developing capable, creative, and responsible learners through computing and engineering education. Our mission is to encourage curiosity, practical skill-building, collaboration, and thoughtful problem-solving.

Our Mission

Create a learning environment where students can strive to grow, ask questions, meet people, and prepare for higher education in the future. Prepare students for the future of technology using cutting edge sciences and a general focus on creation and progress.
Encourage students to collaborate rather than compete with each other
promote experiential learning through hands-on activities and project-based classes.

Our Vision

To foster a learning community recognized for innovation, technical excellence, integrity, and lifelong learning.

Academic standards

Student Evaluation

Students are evaluated through a combination of assessments that measure understanding, practical skills, effort, and the ability to apply concepts. Course-specific grading breakdowns are provided below.

Project-Based Classes

Students are evaluated on independent work, project completion, attendance, and collaboration.

  • Homework — 20%

    Assigned homework, preparation activities, and individual work.

  • Projects — 60%

    Quality, completion, creativity, and overall execution of projects.

  • Attendance — 10%

    Regular attendance and participation in class activities.

  • Teamwork — 10%

    Collaboration, communication, and contribution to group efforts.

Lecture-Based Classes

Students are evaluated on homework, testing performance, and engagement with course material.

  • Homework — 30%

    Homework, practice exercises, and assignments that reinforce course material.

  • Tests — 50%

    Quizzes, exams, and formal assessments measuring understanding.

  • Class Participation — 20%

    Discussions, asking questions, and active engagement during lectures.

Honors Requirements

Students must maintain a grade above 90% in every class and participate in at least one extracurricular club.

Bob Jones Award

Students who maintain these requirements for an entire semester will receive the Bob Jones Award.

Extra Honors

Students must maintain a grade of 95% or higher in every class and hold an officer position in at least one extracurricular club.

Job Bones Award

Students who maintain these requirements for an entire semester will receive the Job Bones Award.

Academic Probation

Students who earn a grade of 55% or lower in at least three of their six classes will be considered failing and placed on academic probation.

Required Tutoring

Students on academic probation are required to attend tutoring sessions to help improve their academic performance.

Community expectations

Code of Conduct

Students are expected to help maintain a respectful, safe, and productive learning environment.

Respect & Professionalism

Treat classmates, instructors, and staff respectfully. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, and intimidation are not acceptable.

Academic Integrity

Submit honest work, credit sources, and follow course rules regarding collaboration, AI tools, and assistance.

Responsible Technology Use

Use institutional systems and technology responsibly. Do not access, disrupt, damage, or misuse systems or data without authorization.

Learning Environment

Follow instructor guidance, participate constructively, and help keep shared learning spaces safe and welcoming.
